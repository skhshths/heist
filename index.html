    How to play

    1. Plan. Pick a crew member and click the blueprint to walk them there. Add waits and actions (grab, hack, ambush) to build their plan. Every crew member's plan runs at the same time.
    2. Check. Drag the timeline to see exactly where everyone will be at any second: your crew, the guards, where the cameras point and which lasers are on. Press Space to watch it move.
    3. Execute. When you're sure, run it. You can't touch anything once it starts. Stay out of every flashlight and camera, never cross a live laser, and don't leave a body where someone will see it.
    4. Escape. Get the score ◆ and everyone to the green exit. Bonus loot ◆ and beating par earn extra stars.
